Llama-2-7B + LoRA — Variance Propagation Architecture
=======================================================
Shapes: B=batch, S=seq_len, H=4096, heads=32, head_dim=128, ffn=11008, r=8

Notation
--------
  [det]   deterministic layer — passes mean unchanged, propagates variance via W²
  [LoRA]  uncertain layer    — LoRA posterior scaled by exp(log_s) before VP
  〈T〉    learnable variance scale applied here (per-sub-block or global)
  (var=0) variance is zero here (deterministic embedding)

│  │                                                                       │

│                                                                          │
│  residual add:   x = x_in2 + mlp_out                                    │
│    mean: x_in2.mean + mlp_out.mean                                       │
│    var:  x_in2.var  + mlp_out.var                                        │

MC predictive:  z ~ N(mean, diag(var))
  p̂(y|x) = E[softmax(z)]  ≈  (1/K) Σ softmax(mean + √var · ε_k),  ε_k ~ N(0,I)

Calibration variants and their learnable parameters
----------------------------------------------------

  last_layer        log_s (1)
                    Backbone runs MAP (var=0 entering lm_head from backbone).
                    Only lm_head LoRA variance is propagated.

  global            log_s (1)  +  log_T scalar (1)
                    Single T applied to both T_attn and T_mlp at every layer.

  per_layer         log_s (1)  +  log_T[32] (32)
                    One T per layer, applied after MLP residual (full layer output).
                    T_attn=None (no scaling after attention residual alone).

  per_sub_block     log_s (1)  +  log_T[32, 2] (64)
                    Separate T_attn[i] and T_mlp[i] per layer.

  per_sub_block_logit  log_s (1)  +  log_T[32, 2] (64)  +  log_T_logit (1)
                    As above, plus a final scale on the logit-level variance.

Two-phase optimisation
----------------------
  Phase 1 (last_layer):  optimise log_s alone  →  identifies global posterior scale
  Phase 2 (backbone variants):  initialise log_s = log_s*  from phase 1
                                 freeze log_s  (or finetune at LR_S_FINETUNE)
                                 optimise log_T  at full LR
